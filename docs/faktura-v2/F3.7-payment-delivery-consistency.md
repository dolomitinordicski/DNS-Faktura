# DNS Faktura v2 — F3.7 Payment & Delivery Consistency

## Principle

Payment and delivery are operational states linked to the exact INVOICED Billing Sheet.

Only material ORDER_CONFIRMATION lines are deliverable.

FAIR, IDM and manual service lines are not physical delivery lines.

## Delivery release

A delivery can be released only when:

1. Billing Sheet is INVOICED
2. PaymentCase belongs to the same Billing Sheet
3. at least one deliverable ORDER_CONFIRMATION line exists
4. if any deliverable line requires prepayment, PaymentCase is PAID

For deliverable lines where prepayment is not required, INVOICED is sufficient.

## Delivery basis

Delivery lines are generated from the invoiced ORDER_CONFIRMATION BillingLines.

Therefore:

confirmedQuantity for delivery = invoiced material quantity

This prevents delivery from drifting away from the billing basis.

## Partial delivery

For every DeliveryLine:

remainingQuantity =
confirmedQuantity - deliveredQuantity

Rules:
- deliveredQuantity cannot be negative
- deliveredQuantity cannot exceed confirmedQuantity
- zero delivered -> PENDING
- partial delivered -> PARTIAL
- all delivered -> DELIVERED

## Scope

Payment status is operational only. Faktura v2 does not perform bank reconciliation or accounting.
